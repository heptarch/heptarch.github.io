**June 22, 2026.** *Bubbles, computational complexity, and my experience with Fable 5.*

## Introduction

Like many people around the world, I was excited to get my hands on Anthropic's frontier model, `Fable 5`, when it came out. For a little while now, my test of frontier models has been simple: formulate and prove a rigorous version of an intuition I have. `Opus 4.8`, `GPT 5.5` and `Gemini 4 (Pro)` all fell short, either proving something erroneous, or mismatching result and intuition. By bouncing `Fable 5` off another model, however, it gave something surprising, novel, and seemingly correct.
