**June 20, 2026.** *What is the error rate of a classical computer?*

## Introduction

Classical computers are very reliable. But one possibility of error the designers probably didn't expect: bombardment by high-energy particles from out of space. These are called *cosmic rays*, and they are mostly protons flung out from the sun at close to the speed of light. We'll do a quick Fermi estimate to check how often they cause a bit to flip in a computer.
