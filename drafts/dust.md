**June 10, 2026.** *Some quantitative meditations upon seeing a dust devil.*

I recently observed a *dust devil* (or small whirlwind) in an empty cul-de-sac on a cool day of gusty winds. The devil was the on the order of the sie of the cul-de-sac, say $L \sim 10 \text{ m}$ across, and moving at around $u = 1 \text{ m/s}$. The dynamical viscosity of air at $T = 15^\circ$ and standard pressure is around $\nu \approx 1.48 \times 10^{-5} \text{ m}^2/\text{s}$.
